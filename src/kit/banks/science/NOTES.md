# Science bank notes — NC Standard Course of Study for Science (adopted July 2023, implemented 2024–25)

18 questions per grade (234 total). Every item is authored with the correct choice first (`answer: 0`).
High school mapping: 9 = Earth & Environmental Science, 10 = Biology, 11 = Chemistry, 12 = Physics.

## Research method and limits
The sandbox egress proxy blocked every standards site tried (dpi.nc.gov, ixl.com, generationgenius.com,
commonstandardsproject.com, jove.com, lifeandscience.org, mysteryscience.com, pltw.org, scribd.com, the
poehealth.org mirror of the DPI PDF). All codes were confirmed through web-search result excerpts from those
pages, not by reading the full DPI document. The 2023 standards largely re-code the 2009 Essential Standards
(for example, 5.L.1 became LS.5.1), and that pattern was used to fill gaps. Every gap is listed below.
