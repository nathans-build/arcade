# Social studies bank notes — NC Standard Course of Study for Social Studies (adopted Feb 4, 2021, implemented 2021–22)

18 questions per grade (234 total). Every item is authored with the correct choice first (`answer: 0`).
High school mapping: 9 = World History, 10 = Founding Principles of the USA and NC: Civic Literacy,
11 = American History, 12 = Economics and Personal Finance.

## Standards format
Codes are `grade.strand.standard.objective`, for example `5.C&G.1.1`. The five content strands are History (H),
Geography (G), Economics (E), Civics & Government (C&G) and Behavioral Sciences (B). There is also an Inquiry (I)
skills strand, which has no items here. High school codes start with a course prefix: `WH.`, `AH.`, `CL.` (Civic
Literacy) and `EPF.`. The EPF course uses its own strands: E (economics), IE (income and education), MM (money
management), FP (financial planning) and CC (critical consumerism).

Where an objective number could not be confirmed, items use the **standard-level** code (for example `3.G.1`).
Where the objective was confirmed, they use the full code (for example `3.G.1.3`).

## High school course placement
The state sets **no required order** for the four graduation courses (NC DPI FAQ). This bank follows the traditional
NC order, which matches the older Essential Standards sequence (9 World History, 10 Civics & Economics,
11–12 American History I/II): 9 WH, 10 Civic Literacy, 11 American History, 12 EPF. To use 10 = American History
and 11 = Civic Literacy instead, swap the contents of `g10.ts` and `g11.ts` and update their `grade` and `id` fields.

## Research method and limits
The sandbox egress proxy blocked dpi.nc.gov, sites.google.com, study.com, ixl.com, appstate.edu, simbli (board
attachments), banzai, ASN, district sites, docs.google.com, newpathworksheets and instructure.com. One source could be
read in full: a 2022–23 district 5th-grade pacing guide (core-docs.s3.amazonaws.com) that quotes every grade 5
objective. All other codes come from web-search result excerpts, including excerpts from DPI's 2022 Extended Content
Standards (ECS) documents, which mirror the standard codes.
