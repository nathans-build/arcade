# ELA question bank: notes

This bank follows the North Carolina Standard Course of Study for English Language Arts. The State Board of Education adopted it in 2017, and schools began using it in 2018-19. It is still the course of study in place for 2026-27.

- There are 13 files (`gK.ts` to `g12.ts`) with 48 questions each, 624 in all.
  - Items 01-18 in each grade are the original bank.
  - Items 19-48 were added in October 2026: 390 new items, 272 of them quick (70%).
- Every question is written with the correct answer first (`answer: 0`). The deck shuffles the choices.
- All passages are original writing. Short quotations are used only from public-domain sources: Shakespeare, Lincoln, Kennedy's inaugural address (a U.S. government work), the Declaration of Independence, the Constitution and Caesar.
- Grades 9-10 use the `9-10` band codes (English I and II). Grades 11-12 use the `11-12` band codes (English III and IV).
- K-2 items are kept short so they can be read aloud. Phonics prompts ask about sounds ("Which word starts with the /sh/ sound?"). Any item that depends on seeing the spelling starts with "Which word…" or "Which spelling…".

## Source used to confirm codes (October 2026)

dpi.nc.gov and files.nc.gov are still blocked from this sandbox. However, the full text of NC DPI's **2023 "Quick Reference Guide for the NC Standard Course of Study, English Language Arts"** for **grades K-8** could be downloaded and read. These guides are reposted by a North Carolina district (LCS) on `core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com`. Every K-8 code in this bank was checked against them.

### Does RL.x.10 / RI.x.10 exist? Yes.

NC keeps a tenth reading standard under the heading "Range of Reading and Level of Complexity". Unlike the Common Core, NC's version adds "Connect prior knowledge and experiences to text." Examples:
- RL.K.10 / RI.K.10: "Actively engage in group reading activities with purpose and understanding."
- RL.3.10: "By the end of grade 3, read and understand literature at the high end of the 2-3 text complexity band proficiently and independently for sustained periods of time. Connect prior knowledge and experiences to text."
- RL.4.10 and RI.4.10 cover the 4-5 complexity band, and RL.6.10 and RI.6.10 cover the 6-8 band.

The .10 standards describe reading stamina and text complexity, which a single multiple-choice item cannot measure. **No item is coded .10**, and the verifier warns if one ever is. A web-search excerpt shows that RL.9-10.10 also exists, but the high-school text itself was not seen.

Two other placeholders in NC's numbering: **RL.x.8 is "Not applicable to literature"** in every grade, and **RI.K.8 "Begins in grade 1"**.

### NC numbering confirmed from the 2023 guides

- **Reading: Foundational Skills (NC differs from Common Core).**
  - K-1:
    - RF.x.1: print concepts. RF.1.1 is "Recognize and use capitalization and ending punctuation".
    - RF.x.2: handwriting.
    - RF.x.3: phonological awareness.
    - RF.x.4: phonics and word analysis.
    - RF.x.5: fluency.
  - Grade 2 has only RF.2.2 (handwriting), RF.2.4 (phonics) and RF.2.5 (fluency). There is **no RF.2.1 or RF.2.3**.
  - Grades 3-5 have only RF.x.2 (handwriting: manuscript and cursive in grade 3, cursive in grades 4-5), **RF.x.4 (phonics and word analysis: prefixes, derivational and Latin suffixes, multisyllabic words)** and **RF.x.5 (fluency)**. There is no RF.3.1 or RF.3.3.
  - **Correction:** the earlier notes guessed that grade 3-5 RF followed Common Core numbering ("RF.3.4 is fluency"). That was wrong. In NC, RF.3.4, RF.4.4 and RF.5.4 are phonics and word analysis, and the new grade 3-5 decoding items use those codes. The verifier rejects RF.2.1, RF.2.3, RF.3-5.1, RF.3-5.3 and any RF.x.2 (handwriting).
- **Language.**
  - L.x.1 (grammar) and L.x.2 (capitalization, punctuation, spelling) point to grade-band continuums: K-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-8 and 9-12. There are no lettered sub-standards. Items were placed using the continuum lists:
    - 2-3: irregular plurals and verbs, reflexive pronouns, comparative adverbs, coordinating and subordinating conjunctions, homophones.
    - 4-5: modal auxiliaries, progressive and perfect tenses, relative pronouns and adverbs, adjective order, correlative conjunctions, fragments and run-ons, and commas for intro words, yes/no, tag questions and direct address.
    - 6-8: pronoun case, intensive pronouns, verbals, voice and mood, transitive and intransitive verbs, misplaced and dangling modifiers, nonrestrictive commas, coordinate adjectives, ellipsis, dash, semicolon, colon, hyphen.
  - L.K.3 and L.1.3 read "(Begins in grade 2)". L.2.3 (formal vs. informal English) is used in grade 2.
  - L.x.4 covers vocabulary strategies: "context clues, word parts, word relationships, and reference materials".
  - L.x.5 sub-standards:
    - K-1: sorting into categories, opposites, and shades of meaning.
    - Grade 2: shades of meaning only.
    - Grade 3: literal vs. nonliteral meaning; degrees of certainty.
    - Grade 4: similes and metaphors; idioms, adages and proverbs; **antonyms and synonyms (L.4.5c)**.
    - Grade 5: figurative language; idioms, adages and proverbs. **There is no word-relationships sub-standard.**
    - Grades 6-8: figures of speech; connotation. **There is no word-relationships sub-standard.**
  - **Recoded existing items.** Because NC moves word relationships into L.x.4, three existing items were recoded:
    - `ela-g6-08`, analogy: L.6.5 → L.6.4.
    - `ela-g7-09`, analogy: L.7.5 → L.7.4.
    - `ela-g5-07`, multiple-meaning word *bat*: L.5.5 → L.5.4.
- **W.x.1** (argument writing) is confirmed for grades 6, 7 and 8 ("Write arguments to support claims with clear reasons and relevant evidence"). W.3.1 is opinion writing.
- **Kindergarten RL/RI** wording was confirmed:
  - RL.K.3: characters, settings and major events.
  - RL.K.5: "Recognize common types of texts".
  - RL.K.6: role of the author and illustrator.
  - RI.K.2: main topic.
  - RI.K.5: front cover, back cover and title page.
- **Grade 1-2 RL/RI** wording was confirmed:
  - RL.1.5: books that tell stories vs. books that give information.
  - RL.1.6: who is telling the story.
  - RI.1.8: the reasons an author gives.
  - RL.2.4: rhythm and meaning.
  - RL.2.6: characters' points of view.
  - RI.2.6: the author's main purpose.
  - RI.2.8: reasons.
- **Grades 3-8 RL/RI 1-9** match the Common Core anchors: 1 evidence and inference, 2 theme or central idea, 3 characters or events, 4 word meaning and tone, 5 structure, 6 point of view or purpose, 7 media, 8 argument (RI only), 9 comparing texts or sources.

## Codes to verify (not seen in a primary NC source)

- **All 9-10 and 11-12 codes.** No NC high-school quick reference guide was found. The codes assume the Common Core numbering, which matches NC for every K-8 code checked. Search excerpts confirm that RL.9-10.5, RL.11-12.5, RL.9-10.10, L.11-12.1 and a single "9-12 grammar continuum" exist. Items that depend most on the Common Core high-school wording:
  - **RI.9-10.9 and RI.11-12.9** (seminal U.S. documents: Declaration, Gettysburg Address, Constitution).
  - **RL.11-12.9** (foundational American literature: Transcendentalism, Romanticism, Harlem Renaissance, Modernism).
  - **L.9-10.3** (writing to a style manual such as MLA). Also confirm that NC kept this clause.
  - **RL.9-10.6** used for narrative point of view. The Common Core wording is "point of view or cultural experience reflected in a work of literature from outside the United States".
- **W codes for grades 9-12** (W.9-10.1, W.11-12.1) are assumed to match grades 6-8.

## How the bank is built and checked

The files are generated, so do not hand-edit them. Edit the generator and regenerate.

Generator and checker (scratchpad, `bank-ela/`):
- `gen.py` holds the original 234 items and applies the three recodes.
- `new_k5.py` and `new_612.py` hold the new items.
- Run `OUT=<banks/ela dir> python3 gen.py`.
- `verify.ts` (`npx tsx verify.ts <dir>`) checks:
  - 48 items per grade and sequential, unique ids.
  - Subject, grade, required fields, four distinct choices, `answer: 0`, no "all/none of the above".
  - Quick limits: prompt ≤ 100 characters and choices ≤ 14 characters with no passage. Any item that fits the limits must be marked quick.
  - At least 2/3 quick per grade and among the new items.
  - Standard code matches the grade or band.
  - NC-specific RF rules; no RL.x.8 codes.
  - Near-duplicate prompts and passages within a grade.
- A cross-grade scan was also run, and duplicate topics were rewritten. The files also pass `tsc --strict` against `kit/types.ts`.
