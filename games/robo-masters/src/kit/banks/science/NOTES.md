## Research method and limits
The sandbox egress proxy blocks dpi.nc.gov and every mirror we tried: poehealth.org (the DPI PDF), ixl.com, albert.io,
stileeducation.com, moreheadplanetarium.org, pltw.org and scribd.com. Codes were confirmed from web-search result
excerpts, which quote objective text from those pages, and never from the full DPI document. The excerpts sometimes
disagree with each other. When they did, the item uses the standard-level code (for example `LS.8.3`) rather than an
objective.

## Fact checks resolved while writing
- **Antacid tablets in water.** The reaction cools the water slightly (it is endothermic), so a planned "fizzes and gets warm" item was dropped.
- **Earth's distance from the sun.** Earth is closest to the sun in early January (perihelion). The item uses this to show that distance does not cause the seasons.
- **Earthquake magnitude.** Each whole magnitude step means 10× the shaking amplitude and about 32× the energy.
- **KNO₃ solubility.** It dissolves about 32 g per 100 g of water at 20 °C and about 110 g at 60 °C, which gives 78 g of crystals in the item.
- **Enzymes.** "Almost all" enzymes are proteins; ribozymes are the exception.
- **Water vapor.** It is worded as "the most abundant greenhouse gas", which is true. The existing item already covers CO₂ as the gas that is increasing from burning fuels.
- **Rutherford item.** The distractor "Solid core" could be read as correct, so it was replaced with "Spread charge".
- **Overlaps with older items.** Items that repeated a fact from an older item in the same grade were replaced:
  - insulin feedback (g10)
  - foam-cup insulation (g6)
  - flat distance-time line and cold-front passage (g7)
  - superposition (g8)
  - empty vs. loaded wagon (g5)
  - homologous limbs (g8)
  - "Which is a learned behavior?" (g5)

## Verification
Scaffolding lives in the session scratchpad `bank-science/`, outside the kit:
- `data/g*.mjs` + `gen.mjs` are the authoring source. They append items 19–48 to the original 18 and apply the code changes above.
- `npx tsx verify.ts` checks that each grade has 48 items and that ids are unique and sequential (`sci-gK-01` … `sci-gK-48`). It checks that subject and grade are right, every required field is non-empty, and every item has 4 distinct choices (compared case- and punctuation-insensitively) with `answer` 0. It rejects "all/none of the above". It checks that quick items meet the limits (prompt ≤ 100 characters, choices ≤ 14, no passage), that each grade has at least 20 new quick items, and that passages are ≤ 350 characters. It checks that standards match the grade's code family (`PS|ESS|LS.<grade>.n[.n]`, `ESS.EES`, `LS.Bio`, `PS.Chm`, `PS.Phy`). It also flags exact and near-duplicate prompts within a grade (word-overlap ≥ 0.6, or ≥ 0.4 with the same answer).
- The 5 remaining near-duplicate warnings were reviewed. Each pair tests different facts with parallel wording: soft/rough, compound/mixture, negative/neutral particle, deep/surface currents, and Boyle's/Charles's law.
- `npx -p typescript tsc -p tsconfig.json` type-checks types.ts and the 13 files with strict, noUnusedLocals and moduleResolution bundler.
