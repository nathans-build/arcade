# Brand and domain name research

Status (October 2026): research only, nothing bought. Needed before any paid version (see
[freemium-plan.md](freemium-plan.md)), because "SpiderBen10" and "Plants vs Undead" lean on other
companies' trademarks.

## How this was checked (October 3, 2026)

- **Domain:** whether the `.com` name resolves in DNS. "Taken" means it resolves, so it is
  registered. "Maybe free" means nothing resolves, which usually but not always means unregistered;
  confirm at a registrar (Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy).
- **Conflicts:** a web search for companies or products already using the name.
- **Not checked yet:** US trademarks (tmsearch.uspto.gov, classes 9 and 41) and social media
  handles.

## Shortlist: no conflict found, `.com` looked free

| Name | Domain | Why | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coin-Op Academy** | coinopacademy.com | "Coin-op" is the classic arcade word: retro arcade + school | Unrelated "Coin Academy" (crypto) and "COIN Education" exist |
| **Quiz Quarter** | quizquarter.com | Arcade quarters + quizzes; short | Sounds more quiz than game |
| **Brightcade** | brightcade.com | Bright + arcade; made-up word, easy to own | Same "-cade" style as Arcademics |
| **Quest Cabinet** | questcabinet.com | Arcade cabinet + quest (fits Page Quest) | Less obviously about learning |
| **Scoreboard Scholars** | scoreboardscholars.com | Clear meaning | Long |
| **NZDO Games** | nzdogames.com (also nzdoarcade.com, nzdoplay.com) | Nathan's initials: original, and keeps him as the creator | Means nothing to strangers on its own |

**Recommendation:** NZDO Games as the studio, with a catchy product name for the arcade, e.g.
"Coin-Op Academy, by NZDO Games".

## Avoid

- **Already used by learning companies:**
  - Scholarcade: an edtech company making learning games for middle and high school.
  - Quizcade: a quiz-game platform.
  - Press Start: Press Start Academy, a games-based learning startup, plus a Scholastic book series.
  - PowerUp: Lexia PowerUp, a major reading product.
  - High Score / HiScore: tutoring companies.
  - Arcademics: established arcade-style learning games for K–8. Avoid look-alikes such as "Arcademy".
- **Crowded:** "Brainy…", "Insert Coin…" (a clothing brand and a game convention), "Level Up
  Scholars" (scholarship programs).
- **Domain registered:**
  - braincade, learncade, smartcade, edcade, studycade, knowcade
  - pixelscholar(s), highscoreacademy, arcadeacademy, learnarcade, knowledgearcade
  - insertknowledge, quarterbrain, quarterquest, joystickgenius, pixelpupils, playeronelearning
  - wisearcade, quarterwise, joystickscholars, arcadium, nzdo.com
