# 80s and 90s game ideas for the arcade

Research date: October 3, 2026. Each idea borrows a **mechanic** from a classic; names, characters
and art must stay original. Name checks were web searches only. Run USPTO and domain checks per
[brand-and-domains.md](brand-and-domains.md) before locking a name.

## Why these picks

- **Subject balance across the 16 games:** math is in 12, science in 11, ELA in 10, and social
  studies in only 3 (Maze Muncher, Jungle Run, Sonar Squad). No game is mainly social studies, and
  only 2 are mainly ELA.
- **The social studies bank is ready:** `kit/banks/social/` now has 48 questions per grade.
- **Genres the arcade doesn't have yet:**
  - detective deduction by reading
  - journey and resource management
  - sim/tycoon
  - physics and aiming
  - push and route puzzles
  - rhythm
  - area claiming
  - card and board games

## Top 12

| # | Working title | Inspired by | Mechanic and learning | Size | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clue Compass** | Where in the World Is Carmen Sandiego? (1985) | Detective chase. Reading-inference clues decide where to fly next (the 4 destinations are the multiple choice). Social studies by grade: community, then NC, US, world, and history eras. Plus ELA inference. | M | No red coat or fedora, no ACME/V.I.L.E. Needs a table of place facts per band. |
| 2 | **Wagon Road South** | The Oregon Trail (1985) + Lemonade Stand | A family wagon on the real Great Wagon Road into the NC Piedmont. Rationing teaches division and rates; the trading post teaches money; landmarks teach NC history (4.H.1, 8.H.1). | L (M for one route) | No hunting and no deaths. Show Native nations respectfully, with a history review. Overlaps Family Road Trip's NC theme. |
| 3 | **Equal Pop** | Puzzle Bobble (1994) | Bubble shooter where bubbles pop when *equal*: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, synonyms, same science category. | M | Labels must fit in about 16 px bubbles. |
| 4 | **Spark Path** | Pipe Dream (1989) | Lay circuit tiles before the current flows; insulators break it; series and parallel later (PS.4.2). Water-cycle and number-pipeline variants. | M | K–2 needs a no-timer mode. |
| 5 | **Splash Arc** | Gorillas (1991), Scorched Earth | Set angle and power to lob water at rooftop campfires and gardens. Angles (NC.4.MD.5–7), gravity (PS.5.2.1), projectiles in physics. | **S** | Never target people or animals. |
| 6 | **Crate Clause** | Sokoban (1982) | Push word or number crates so the row reads as a true sentence or equation (L.x.1, order of operations, balancing chemical equations). | M | Level design is the cost; needs undo. |
| 7 | **Fence Fill** | Qix (1981) | Fence off exactly 3/4 or 60% of a meadow. Area, fractions and percent. | S–M | Line-drawing on touch. |
| 8 | **Sidewalk Stand** | Lemonade Stand (1979) + Tapper (1983) | Juice cart: forecast, budget and price, then a serving rush where you make change. Economics (K.E.1 to EPF). | M | Not a bar; keep upgrades small. |
| 9 | **Timeline Trail** | Blockade / Nibbler (1982) | A train couples cars in the right order: history events, story order, life cycles, number lines. | S | Needs a new `sequence` question type in the kit. |
| 10 | **Syllable Jam** | PaRappa (1996), Simon, DDR | 4-lane rhythm game on keys 1–4: syllables, rhymes, poetic meter, skip-counting chants. | M | Audio lag on iPad needs calibration; offer a visual mode. |
| 11 | **Creek Chain** | Odell Lake (1986) | Be a fish in an NC food web and choose eat, flee or ignore (LS.5.2, energy pyramids). | M | Keep it gentle, a "gulp" and never blood. |
| 12 | **Ledge Leaper** | Super Mario Bros. (1985), Word Rescue (1992) | Platformer where you carry word or number tiles to the matching picture or number-line spot. | L | Highest look-alike risk; precise jumps are hard on touch. |

**Also considered, ranked lower:** Lemmings, SimCity, The Incredible Machine, Lunar Lander, racing,
Rampart, Breakout, Zelda-style, Number Munchers, Math Blaster, Reader Rabbit, Mixed-Up Mother Goose
(better as a "Page Quest Jr."), sports, scrolling shooters, Dr. Mario/Columns, Wumpus/Mastermind, and
Concentration. They were too big, duplicated existing games, or were weak on touch.

## Suggested build order

1. **Clue Compass:** fills the biggest gap (social studies plus reading), and the bank is ready.
2. **Splash Arc:** small, fast, and unlike anything in the arcade.
3. **Syllable Jam:** more ELA, and the first music game.
4. **Wagon Road South**, or **Sidewalk Stand** if Family Road Trip ships first.

Next after these: Spark Path, Equal Pop, Timeline Trail.
